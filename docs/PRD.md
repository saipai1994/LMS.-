# Product requirements (summary)
Multi-tenant K-12 ERP + LMS for day and boarding schools: admissions, SIS, academics/LMS, attendance, hostel and dismissal safety, finance and accounting, transport, library, inventory, HR/payroll, health, security, communications, reports, privacy, with mandatory deterministic demo mode (Greenwood International Academy).
Status per requirement: `REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md`. Constraints: real persistence, server-side authz, tenant isolation, audit trails, honest limitations, no fake integrations.
